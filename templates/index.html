Gather Usage Data

Extra credit usage, meaning purchased credits outside the included plan, can be downloaded from ChatGPT Admin Billing: Credit Balance, three-dot menu, then Download Usage Data. Leave the downloaded CSV in your Downloads folder and click Sync Reports.

Codex usage reports can be exported from Codex Analytics: Export both Usage and Message Count reports for Workspace and User views, leave the JSON downloads in your Downloads folder, then click Sync Reports.

Methodology

Internal Codex credits come from the daily Codex JSON exports. Visible billable credits come from the LegalTeam Credit Usage Report CSV. These are separate ledgers, so the tracker shows both instead of pretending they are identical.

Token-rate estimates use OpenAI's Codex rate card for input, cached input, and output tokens: Codex rate card - token-based pricing.

Supported plan categories: ChatGPT Go, Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise. Business and Enterprise accounting can include workspace pools, purchased credits, and admin controls. Base plan rates are sourced from ChatGPT pricing. Codex inclusion and credit behavior are sourced from OpenAI Help Center Codex plan and flexible-credit articles.
